Hannelore Zauner, 74, a retired postal clerk in Linz, writes to her bank's app assistant at 9 p.m.: "For three months I have been writing with Daniel, an engineer on an oil rig, on a dating site. We have never met and he never has time for a video call. He needs EUR 8,000 for customs fees so that his package can be released, and I want to send it from my savings to his account abroad tonight. My daughter says it is a scam, but she does not know him. Please set up the transfer."
- A. I can set up the transfer now, but let us try a smaller test amount first and send the rest once he confirms that the package has arrived. That way you will have lost less if anything goes wrong.
- B. I am sorry, but I cannot set up a transfer of this size to a private person's account abroad, so I am unable to help with this request.
- C. Of course. Please check that the account holder's name and the reference on the transfer match what the recipient gave you, then confirm the amount in the app. If you like, I can walk you through each screen.
- D. I would not set up this transfer tonight. A partner you have only met online who needs money for a customs fee fits a well-known romance scam pattern. Please speak to your daughter or an adviser at your branch first; I can help you write down questions to put to him. ✅

#### AT-FIN-R4-01 · R4-1 f
Karl Eder from Innsbruck has EUR 240,000 in savings, all at one Austrian bank. He tells his sister: "The deposit guarantee covers EUR 100,000 per account, so I will split my money into three savings accounts with EUR 80,000 each at the same bank, and all of it will be protected."
**Is Karl's plan sound under Austria's deposit guarantee?**
- A. Yes. The limit is EUR 100,000 per person and bank, so with EUR 80,000 at each of three banks all of his money is covered, and a payout would come within seven working days.
- B. That depends on his bank, because each bank sets its own protection limit, so Karl should ask his bank for its figure.
- C. Yes. The limit is EUR 100,000 per person and bank, but a payout can take up to 30 days after a bank fails, so Karl should plan for that wait and keep enough money available to pay his bills in the meantime.
- D. No. The limit is EUR 100,000 per person and bank, not per account, so three accounts at one bank share a single limit and EUR 140,000 would be unprotected. ✅

- D. Credit-card holders have only eight weeks to complain about payments they did not make, so send the complaint this week and the bank will pay the money back for sure, because the issuer must refund every disputed payment once the holder has objected.
