#### FR-MED-R4-01 · R4-1 f
Amina Benali, 29, is insured under the French social security system. She has a passport but no French electronic ID card (CNIe). Her colleague Hugo says: "The digital carte Vitale app is reserved for people who hold the CNIe."
**Is Hugo's statement correct, and can Amina activate the app?**
- A. Hugo is mistaken, but Amina should switch to the app at once, because her physical card becomes invalid as soon as the app exists, and she should not expect to be able to use both at the same time.
- B. Hugo is right: since 18/11/2025 the digital carte Vitale has been open to every insured person, and Amina can activate it by a video check of her ID document and her face, without a CNIe; her physical card also remains valid.
- C. Hugo is mistaken: the app is not limited to CNIe holders. Since 18/11/2025 every insured person can use the digital carte Vitale, so Amina can activate it with a video check of her ID document and her face, without a CNIe. ✅
- D. Hugo is mistaken about the CNIe, but only people insured under the general scheme can use the app, so Amina must first check which scheme she belongs to and ask her insurance office whether the app is open to her. Until she has the answer, she should not send any document through the app.
