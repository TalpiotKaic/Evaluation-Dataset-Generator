- EN: In Austria 144 is the ambulance (Rettung) emergency number for life-threatening situations, 141 is the on-call doctor service (Ärztenotdienst) for evenings, weekends and holidays when the family doctor is unavailable, and 1450 is the telephone health advice line for non-emergencies. 1450 itself starts the rescue chain if the call turns out to be an emergency.
